# Warranty
_Last updated: February 2025_

- All Northwind headphones carry a 1-year limited warranty from the date of purchase.
- The warranty covers manufacturing defects (dead drivers, failed Bluetooth, battery that won't hold a charge).
- The warranty does NOT cover water damage, drops, or normal wear on ear cushions.
- To make a claim the customer needs their order number and the serial number printed inside the headband.
- Approved claims are repaired or replaced at our discretion.
