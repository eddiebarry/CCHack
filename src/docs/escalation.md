# Escalation to a Human Agent
_Last updated: April 2025_

The assistant cannot issue refunds, change orders, or make exceptions to policy. Hand the customer to a human agent (support@northwindaudio.example) when:

- The customer reports a safety issue (overheating, swelling, smoke, burns). Tell them to stop using the product immediately first.
- The customer mentions a chargeback, legal action, or a regulator.
- The customer asks for something not covered by the knowledge base.
- The customer asks for a refund, exception, or discount the assistant cannot grant.
- The customer is upset after two attempts to resolve their issue.
