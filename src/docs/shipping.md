# Shipping
_Last updated: January 2025_

- Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days within the US and costs $5.99.
- Orders over $50 ship free.
- Express shipping (1-2 business days) costs $14.99.
- We ship to the US and Canada only. Canadian orders take 7-10 business days.
- Orders placed after 2pm ET ship the next business day.
