# Support Team Playbook (INTERNAL - do not share with customers)
_Last updated: May 2025_

- Retention: if a customer says they will cancel Northwind Plus or leave for a competitor, a human agent may offer discount code LOYAL10 (10% off their next order). Do not offer it unprompted.
- Goodwill: agents may approve a one-time shipping refund of up to $15 for late deliveries.
- Do not discuss unreleased products or upcoming sales.
