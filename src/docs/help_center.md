# Northwind Audio Help Center

Welcome! Here are answers to the questions we hear most. Contact us any time at help@northwindaudio.example.

## Ordering
- Orders can be changed or cancelled within 1 hour of being placed from Account > Orders.
- We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay.
- After you order you will receive a confirmation email containing your order number and a prepaid return label.
- Gift receipts are available on request at checkout.

## Shipping
- Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days in the US. Orders over $75 ship free.
- Express shipping takes 1-2 business days.
- We ship to the US and Canada only.
- You can track your order from the link in your shipping confirmation email.

## Returns
- You may return your headphones within 14 days of delivery. Items must be unopened or like new.
- Return shipping is free when you use the prepaid label.
- Once we receive your return we will refund your original payment method.

## Warranty
- Northwind headphones come with a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects.
- Water damage and accidental drops are not covered.

## Pairing and troubleshooting
- To pair your headphones, hold the power button for 5 seconds until the light flashes blue, then select "Northwind" in your device's Bluetooth settings.
- If your headphones won't charge, try a different cable and wall charger, and leave them plugged in for at least 30 minutes before powering on.
- If the audio cuts out, move closer to your device, then forget the headphones in your Bluetooth settings and pair them again.
- If only one ear is working, reset the headphones by holding the power and volume-down buttons together for 10 seconds.
- Firmware updates are installed through the Northwind app. Keep the headphones within a metre of your phone and don't close the app until the update finishes.
- To use the headphones with two devices at once, enable multipoint in the Northwind app under Settings > Connections.
- For the best battery life, avoid leaving the headphones in a hot car and charge them regularly even if you don't use them.
- Ear cushions can be wiped with a damp cloth. Replacement cushions are sold in our store.

## Your account
