# Northwind Plus Subscription
_Last updated: April 2025_

- Northwind Plus costs $9.99/month or $99/year and includes free express shipping and extended returns (60 days).
- Customers can cancel anytime from Account > Subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.
- Monthly payments are not prorated or refunded after the billing date.
- Annual plans can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase or renewal.
